⚡ Butterfly Bloom Overview

  • Access Requirement: Complete the 3-stage Wisp Questline in the Enchanted Forest.
  • Core Gameplay: Equip your Butterfly Net, sprint around the floral dome, and click/tap to catch flying butterflies.
  • Rarest Catch: The Ethereal Prism Butterfly (0.5% spawn rate), which grants guaranteed Enchanted Pet Mutations.
EVENT MECHANICS

How the Butterfly Bloom Event Works

The Butterfly Bloom is the premier endgame feature of Update 7. Once the Enchanted Tree's barrier has dissolved, you can enter the hollow trunk chamber at any time.

🦋 Step-by-Step Catching Walkthrough:

  1. Equip the Net: Select the Butterfly Net from your hotbar after entering the tree dome.
  2. Watch for Swarm Waves: Butterflies spawn in recurring waves every few minutes, hovering at various heights above the glowing flowers.
  3. Jump & Net: Use your character's speed to leap toward airborne targets and click to swing your net.
  4. Collect Rewards: Caught specimens instantly convert into Forest Essence or Mutation catalysts.

PET BOOSTING

What is the Enchanted Mutation?

The Enchanted Mutation is a permanent pet upgrade consumable obtained exclusively from high-tier butterflies. Applying it to any equipped pet increases its base speed output by up to +50% without resetting its level.

Save for Secret & Mythic Pets

Never waste Enchanted Mutations on common or starter pets. Reserve them for your highest base multiplier companions.

Stack with Rebirth Multipliers

Pet mutation bonuses compound multiplicatively with your character's rebirth level, drastically cutting the time to future speed milestones.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Butterfly Bloom FAQ

Can other players steal butterflies from your net?

Butterfly spawns are shared in the public server, so higher speed players can catch them first. Joining a smaller server makes catching easier.

Do I need to repeat the Wisp quest to enter the tree again?

No. Once you complete the Wisp questline once, the Enchanted Tree remains permanently unlocked on your account.
