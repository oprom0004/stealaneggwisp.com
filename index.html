STEP 1: LOCATION & UNLOCK

Where to Find the Wisp NPC in Steal An Egg

In Update 7, the developers introduced the Enchanted Forest biome. Unlike earlier biomes, you cannot immediately walk in unless your character has reached the massive 50 Billion (50,000,000,000) Speed milestone.

📍 Wisp Exact Location:

  1. Train to 50B Speed: Equip your best multiplier pets and run on the highest available treadmill.
  2. Enter the Enchanted Forest Portal: Pass through the 50B Speed gateway at the end of the previous biome.
  3. Locate Wisp: Wisp is a glowing, ethereal spirit NPC floating near the center clearing directly facing the giant mystical locked tree.
STEP 2: QUESTLINE WALKTHROUGH

Wisp Quests: How to Open the Enchanted Tree

Interacting with Wisp initiates a multi-stage questline. You must complete every objective before the root barriers around the Enchanted Tree dissolve.

Quest Stage Objective Tips & Strategy
Stage 1: Egg Retrieval Steal and hatch designated Enchanted Eggs Use high-tier speed boosts to dodge defender sentries quickly.
Stage 2: Pet Fusion Perform fusions using the secret fusion machine Combine lower-tier forest pets to fulfill fusion requirements.
Stage 3: Tree Awakening Return to Wisp with the required catalysts Interact with Wisp to trigger the cutscene opening the Enchanted Tree.

🦋 What Happens Inside the Enchanted Tree? (Butterfly Bloom)

Once inside, you gain access to the exclusive Butterfly Bloom Event:

  • Equip the Butterfly Net tool;
  • Catch floating enchanted butterflies spawning inside the tree dome;
  • Earn the rare Enchanted Mutation consumable to supercharge your pet multiplier stats!
FASTEST PROGRESSION

How to Reach 50 Billion Speed Fast

Hitting 50B Speed without optimal pet stacking can take days. Follow this proven progression route:

1. Auto-Hatch Best Multiplier Pets

Always hatch the most expensive egg you can afford. Stack pets with high base speed percentages over vanity cosmetics.

2. Utilize Rebirth Multipliers

Rebirthing resets your current speed but gives a permanent multiplier. Time your rebirths whenever speed gains begin to plateau.

3. Treadmill Tier Optimization

Never train on starter treadmills. Always sprint on the highest treadmill unlocked in your current farthest biome.
